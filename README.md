# Chomp Man

## About the Game
Chomp Man is an arcade-style maze that's similar to the classic Pac-Man. You play as a character chomping pellets to rack up the score, but you will be hunted by ghosts. Best of luck since it may end up chaotic. At random intervals after eating a power pellet, your controls may end up inverted to disorient you. Don't let the ghost hunters catch you while you get disoriented!

---

## How to Play

**Controls:** WASD / Arrow Keys *(Controls may go inverted)*

**Objective:**
Collect as many points as possible by eating all the pellets in the maze. Avoid the ghosts or you'll lose a life. Survive as long as you can and go for a high score!

---

## Jam Theme Integration
The way I interpreted the theme "Switch" was that your controls would switch randomly at some point mid-game, throwing you off your toes and challenging you to think critically at some points in the game. Chomping away is that you become the hunter and the ghosts become the hunted, which depends on which mode you are in!
